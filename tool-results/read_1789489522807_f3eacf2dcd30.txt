   147→   147→

   154→   154→
   155→   155→architecture.
   156→   156→
   157→   157→## `StaffOnboarding`
   158→   158→
   159→   159→One onboarding record per Staff member.
   160→   160→
   161→   161→Required responsibilities:
   162→   162→
   163→   163→* Staff relation
   164→   164→* onboarding content version
   165→   165→* current/resume step
   166→   166→* started timestamp
   167→   167→* completed timestamp
   168→   168→* created/updated timestamps
   169→   169→
   170→   170→Use:
   171→   171→
   172→   172→```text
   173→   173→staffId @unique
   174→   174→```
   175→   175→
   176→   176→to guarantee one onboarding journey per Staff record.
   177→   177→
   178→   178→---
   179→   179→
   180→   180→## `StaffOnboardingStep`
   181→   181→
   182→   182→Persist explicit completion of individual onboarding steps.
   183→   183→
   184→   184→Required responsibilities:
   185→   185→
   186→   186→* parent onboarding relation
   187→   187→* stable config `stepId`
   188→   188→* completion timestamp
   189→   189→
   190→   190→Use a unique constraint equivalent to:
   191→   191→
   192→   192→```text
   193→   193→@@unique([onboardingId, stepId])
   194→   194→```
   195→   195→
   196→   196→This gives:
   197→   197→
   198→   198→* resume
   199→   199→* auditability
   200→   200→* exactly-once step completion
   201→   201→* concurrency protection
   202→   202→
   203→   203→Do NOT store completed steps as an untyped JSON array.
   204→   204→
