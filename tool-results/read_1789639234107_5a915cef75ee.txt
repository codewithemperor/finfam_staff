   115→## 1. "Established Member" savings threshold — RESOLVED (₦5,000)
   116→
   117→The source material originally defined an *Established Member* with
   118→**different savings thresholds in different sections** (₦2,000 vs ₦5,000).
   119→
   120→- **Where it appears:** reward/policy sections of the recruitment material.
   121→- **Why it mattered:** the threshold determines eligibility language and
   122→  reward communication on the landing page, in the Responsibility & Reward
   123→  Agreement (offer stage), and possibly in assessment content.
   124→- **Resolution:** the **Provisional Letter of Appointment dated September 1,
   125→  2026 (Ref: FFL/HR/PLA/012)** confirms **₦5,000**. A member becomes
   126→  *Established* when, during their first month, they (1) complete membership
   127→  signup, (2) make their first deposit, and (3) maintain savings of not less
   128→  than ₦5,000. The earlier ₦2,000 figure is superseded and must not appear
   129→  in any content going forward.
   130→- **Status:** ✅ Resolved. Published in Agreement v1.1
   131→  (`src/config/agreement.ts` → reward structure). The landing page
   132→  (`src/config/content.ts`) and orientation section 5
