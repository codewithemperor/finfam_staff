   179→
   180→## `StaffOnboardingStep`
   181→
   182→Persist explicit completion of individual onboarding steps.
   183→
   184→Required responsibilities:
   185→
   186→* parent onboarding relation
   187→* stable config `stepId`
   188→* completion timestamp
   189→

   191→
   192→```text
   193→@@unique([onboardingId, stepId])
   194→```
   195→
   196→This gives:
   197→
   198→* resume
   199→* auditability
   200→* exactly-once step completion
   201→* concurrency protection
   202→
   203→Do NOT store completed steps as an untyped JSON array.
