# 3. APPROVED ONBOARDING DATA MODEL

Implement the planned:

```text
StaffOnboarding
StaffOnboardingStep
```

architecture.

## `StaffOnboarding`

One onboarding record per Staff member.

Required responsibilities:

* Staff relation
* onboarding content version
* current/resume step
* started timestamp
* completed timestamp
* created/updated timestamps

Use:

```text
staffId @unique
```

to guarantee one onboarding journey per Staff record.

---

## `StaffOnboardingStep`

Persist explicit completion of individual onboarding steps.

Required responsibilities:

* parent onboarding relation
* stable config `stepId`
* completion timestamp

Use a unique constraint equivalent to:

```text
@@unique([onboardingId, stepId])
```

This gives:

* resume
* auditability
* exactly-once step completion
* concurrency protection

Do NOT store completed steps as an untyped JSON array.
