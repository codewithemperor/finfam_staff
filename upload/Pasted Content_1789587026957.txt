Stage 11 should give authorized FinFam management personnel a secure operational interface for managing the Staff Platform that already exists.

Potential responsibilities include:

* viewing Staff records
* viewing onboarding status
* viewing Staff Training progress
* viewing Daily Reports
* viewing Appointment/Staff identity information
* suspending/reactivating Staff Portal access
* managing Staff Resources in the future
* correcting operational records only where policy permits
* maintaining an auditable record of privileged actions

* recruitment processing
* member CRM
* earnings/payroll
* performance scoring
* target ranking
* authoritative member verification
* financial administration

# 4. AUTHENTICATION VS AUTHORIZATION

> Who are you?

> What are you allowed to do?

Even if Admin uses the same email OTP mechanism, privileged authorization must be explicit.

Do NOT treat:

```text
having a StaffAccount
```

as equivalent to:

```text
having admin authority
```

* `/staff/*` inaccessible

* Daily Reports inaccessible
* Training inaccessible
